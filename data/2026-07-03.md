### [1] [Wormholes as red herrings: reflection positivity and the reconstruction of unitary quantum field theories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01322)
*Jacob McNamara,Zhencheng Wang*

Main category: hep-th

TL;DR: 本文证明，量子引力中因时空虫洞（爱因斯坦-罗森桥）导致的希尔伯特空间分解的表观失效，实则源于对带电态谱的限制，而非真正的分解问题。作者给出了量子场论的重构定理：幺正QFT由其闭流形配分函数唯一确定（至幺正同构），每个反射正配分函数都对应一个幺正QFT，且流形制备的态张成重构理论对称群下的不变态空间。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 澄清量子引力中希尔伯特空间分解的表观失效问题，证明其与欧几里得虫洞情形类似，并非根本性困难，而是源于对带电态谱的不完全考虑。

Method: 通过建立量子场论的重构定理，证明幺正QFT由其闭流形配分函数唯一确定，反射正配分函数必对应幺正QFT，且流形制备的态张成对称群不变态空间，从而在引力解释中说明希尔伯特空间分解失效只是假象。

Result: 得到了一个QFT重构定理：幺正QFT由其闭流形配分函数唯一确定（至幺正同构）；反射正配分函数必来自幺正QFT；流形制备的态张成重构理论对称群下的不变态空间。引力解释表明，希尔伯特空间分解的表观失效源于对带电态谱的不完全限制。

Conclusion: 量子引力中希尔伯特空间分解的表观失效并非真正的物理问题，与Coleman关于欧几里得虫洞的论证类似，它源于对带电态谱的不完整考虑，从而消除了空间虫洞带来的理论疑难。

Abstract: As Coleman famously argued, the apparent breakdown of partition-function factorization in quantum gravity associated with Euclidean wormholes is a red herring, arising from a hidden average over an ensemble of theories. We present a direct analog of Coleman's argument for the apparent breakdown of Hilbert-space factorization associated with spatial wormholes, i.e., Einstein--Rosen bridges. Our main result is the following reconstruction theorem for quantum field theories: unitary QFTs are determined, up to unitary isomorphism, by their closed-manifold partition functions; every reflection-positive partition function arises from a unitary quantum field theory; and the states prepared by manifolds span the space of invariant states under the reconstructed theory's symmetry group. Interpreting the result gravitationally, we conclude that any apparent breakdown of Hilbert-space factorization is a red herring, arising from restricting to an incomplete spectrum of charged states.

### [2] [BRST-BV approach to fields in Poincare patch of AdS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02495)
*R. R. Metsaev*

Main category: hep-th

TL;DR: 本文利用AdS空间的庞加莱参数化，发展了一套适用于自由场的通用BRST-BV方法，得到了任意质量和对称类型场的BRST-BV拉格朗日量通用表达式，并将其应用于全对称无质量、有质量、部分有质量任意整数自旋场以及连续自旋场，建立了有约束和无约束两种BRST-BV形式体系，证明了所得拉格朗日量与修正de Donder散度度规定义的度规-like拉格朗日量等价，并在场-反场空间中实现了AdS空间对称性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在AdS空间中系统建立适用于任意质量和对称类型自由场的通用BRST-BV形式体系，弥补已有方法在处理不同自旋场时的碎片化缺陷，并实现与度规-like拉格朗日量的统一匹配。

Method: 采用AdS空间的庞加莱参数化，构建通用BRST-BV框架；分别针对全对称无质量、有质量、部分有质量整数自旋场及连续自旋场，发展有约束与无约束两种BRST-BV形式；通过修正de Donder散度条件建立与度规-like拉格朗日量的对应关系；在场-反场空间中实现AdS对称性的代数表示。

Result: 得到了适用于任意质量和对称类型自由场的通用BRST-BV拉格朗日量表达式；建立了全对称无质量、有质量、部分有质量整数自旋场及连续自旋场的有约束与无约束BRST-BV形式体系；证明了BRST-BV拉格朗日量与基于修正de Donder散度的度规-like拉格朗日量之间的等价性；在场和反场空间中给出了AdS空间对称性的一种实现。

Conclusion: 所提出的通用BRST-BV方法能够统一描述AdS空间中多种类型的自由场，且与度规-like表述等价，为后续研究AdS空间中的量子化及相互作用问题提供了系统的形式框架。

Abstract: We use the Poincare parametrization of AdS space to develop a general BRST-BV approach for free fields. A general expression for the BRST-BV Lagrangian of fields with arbitrary masses and symmetry types is obtained. We apply this general framework to study totally symmetric massless, massive, and partially-massless fields with arbitrary integer spin and a continuous-spin field. For these fields, both the constrained and unconstrained BRST-BV formulations are developed. In addition, we demonstrate the matching between the obtained BRST-BV Lagrangian and the metric-like Lagrangian formulated in terms of the modified de Donder divergence. Finally, a realization of AdS space symmetries is obtained within the space of fields and antifields entering the BRST-BV formulation.

### [3] [Mechanistic Interpretability and Causal Feature Steering of Neural Quantum States via Sparse Autoencoders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01336)
*Zihao Qi,Christopher Earls*

Main category: quant-ph

TL;DR: 本文使用稀疏自编码器分析神经量子态（NQS）的内部激活，发现其无监督地编码了与物理可观测量（如序参量、交错磁化强度等）高度相关的特征，并且通过后训练干预单一特征可平滑地控制相应物理量，证明NQS具有可解释的内部物理表示。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 神经量子态（NQS）虽能有效近似量子多体波函数，但其内部工作机制尚不明确——为何仅在变分目标上训练的NQS能准确捕获从未显式优化的物理可观测量。本文旨在打开NQS的"黑箱"，揭示其内部表示与物理量之间的关系。

Method: 采用稀疏自编码器从NQS的残差流中提取特征，分析这些特征与物理可观测量（序参量、交错磁化强度、半链关联函数）的相关性，并通过后训练干预实验检验特征的因果作用。

Result: 发现提取的特征与物理可观测量高度相关，且该发现完全无监督（未使用物理标签）。对单一特征进行后训练干预可平滑单调地控制相应可观测量，同时几乎不改变变分能量。

Conclusion: NQS不仅是函数逼近器，其内部编码了丰富且可解释的物理信息表示。该方法为NQS提供了诊断和干预工具，为迈向更可靠、透明的NQS奠定了可解释性基础。

Abstract: Neural Quantum States (NQS) are a remarkably expressive class of variational ansätze for quantum many-body wavefunctions, yet little is understood about their internal mechanisms: trained on variational objectives alone, how do NQS accurately capture physical observables that they have never been explicitly optimized for? In this work, we present a systematic approach to analyze the internal activations of NQS using sparse autoencoders. We extract features from the residual stream and demonstrate that these features strongly correlate with physical observables such as order parameters, staggered magnetization, and half-chain correlators, across both ground state representation and real-time dynamics. Remarkably, the discovery of these features is entirely unsupervised, with no physical labels provided. We further establish that such features causally affect the corresponding observables predicted by NQS, by showing that targeted, post-training intervention on a \textit{single} feature smoothly and monotonically steers the corresponding observable, while leaving the variational energy nearly unchanged. These results demonstrate that NQS are not merely functional approximators, but encode rich, interpretable internal representations of physical information. Our approach provides both a diagnostic and an intervention tool for NQS, and serves as a foundation for using mechanistic interpretability towards more reliable, transparent NQS.

### [4] [Ravines in quantum cost landscapes: opportunities for improved VQA predictions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01329)
*Felix J. Beckmann,João F. Bravo*

Main category: quant-ph

TL;DR: 本文利用改进的弹性带算法(NEB)系统分析了变分量子算法中量子代价景观的峡谷结构——连接局部极小值的低代价路径，并基于此构建了集成预测框架。研究发现，基于量子神经网络的NEB集成方法在分类纠缠检测任务中优于经典和朴素量子方法，同时显著降低计算成本。峡谷结构在不同电路深度和量子比特规模下均保持稳定。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 量子代价景观的几何与拓扑结构支配着变分量子算法的优化过程及其预测能力，然而现有研究对代价景观中连接局部极小值的低代价路径（峡谷结构）缺乏系统性分析。理解并利用这些结构对于提升VQA性能至关重要。

Method: 采用源自理论化学的弹性带算法(NEB)的改进版本，系统识别硬件高效拟设的量子代价景观中的峡谷结构。通过训练量子神经网络对量子态的集中纠缠度进行分类，沿低代价NEB路径构建集成预测框架，并提出基于局部预测变异性的轻量级预训练度量指标。

Result: 当基分类器来自具有高局部预测变异性的电路和权重初始化时，基于量子的NEB集成方法优于经典和朴素量子替代方案。复杂性分析表明，利用QCL的峡谷结构可大幅降低计算成本。深度和量子比特规模分析证实峡谷结构在不同规模下均存在，且NEB方法在量子比特规模增大时代价更低、收敛更快。

Conclusion: 峡谷结构是量子代价景观中普遍存在的几何特征，利用该结构构建集成模型不仅能提升预测性能，还能显著降低计算开销。提出的局部预测变异性指标可作为VQA性能的有效预示器。

Abstract: The geometric and topological structure of quantum cost landscapes (QCLs) governs the optimization and thus the predictive power of variational quantum algorithms (VQAs). We systematically analyze ravines - low-cost paths connecting local minima - using an adapted version of the nudged elastic band (NEB) algorithm, a method originating from theoretical chemistry. By training quantum neural networks (QNNs) to classify the concentratable entanglement of quantum states, we apply the NEB algorithm and numerically identify ravine structures in QCLs of hardware-efficient ansatzes. Beyond visualizing these ravines, we construct an ensemble prediction framework by averaging predictions from QNNs parameterized along the low-cost NEB path. We introduce a resource-light pre-training metric which quantifies local-prediction variability and serves as a strong performance indicator for VQAs, even beyond the scope of this study. When base classifiers are drawn from circuit and weight initializations exhibiting high local-prediction variability, the quantum-based NEB ensembles outperform both classical and naive quantum alternatives. Moreover, a complexity analysis shows that leveraging the ravine-like structure of QCLs with the QNN NEB approach substantially reduces computational costs compared to naive QNN ensembling. A depth and qubit scaling analysis indicates that ravines persist across both scalings, and that, despite the expected growth in resource requirements with the qubit scaling, the NEB approach also accelerates convergence over the naive alternative.

</details>
