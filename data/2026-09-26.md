### [1] [Renormalon Saddles in the OPE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28638)
*Arindam Bhattacharya,Jordan Cotler,Aurélien Dersy,Matthew D. Schwartz*

Main category: hep-th

TL;DR: 本文在二维Gross–Neveu模型大N极限下，为算符乘积展开中renormalon模糊性的抵消给出了一个复围道几何实现：微扰Wilson系数与非微扰凝聚态分别对应同一约化被积函数的互补相对围道，其复尾部相消后重建原始实积分围道。

Motivation: 通常renormalon模糊性的抵消被表述为微扰Borel模糊性与非微扰凝聚态方案依赖性之间的匹配，缺乏清晰的几何图像。作者希望在可精确求解的二维Gross–Neveu模型大N极限中，为该抵消机制给出显式的围道实现，并厘清renormalon鞍点、Stokes不连续性与红外Landau极点之间的关系。

Method: 在大N极限下取有质量真空中的精确1/N阶费米子自能，其为一个有限的实积分；仅当将大动量行为分解为微扰Wilson系数与相应算符矩阵元之积后才出现模糊性。作者构造约化积分以分离领头renormalon，并揭示其二维围道几何：微扰级数由交换动量达到外部硬标度的边界角点生成，其Stokes不连续性由零交换动量处的另一临界点控制。随后对凝聚态标度积分的紫外发散进行重整化，在不借助微扰扇区匹配的条件下导出其补偿性横向模糊，并区分renormalon鞍点与表观红外Landau极点；同时从大N辅助场路径积分获得约化围道变量。

Result: renormalon鞍点被识别为该约化模空间积分的边界临界点；微扰Wilson系数与重整化凝聚态定义了同一约化被积函数的互补相对围道，其复尾部相互抵消，从而重建出原始的实积分围道，实现了模糊性抵消的围道几何图像。

Conclusion: 该工作表明renormalon模糊性的抵消可在二维Gross–Neveu模型中理解为同一约化被积函数的互补围道配对，为OPEC中微扰与非微扰贡献的匹配提供了几何化诠释，并明确了renormalon鞍点与红外Landau极点的区别。

Abstract: The cancellation of renormalon ambiguities in the operator product expansion is usually formulated as a matching between perturbative Borel ambiguities and prescription dependence in non-perturbative condensates. We give this cancellation a contour realization in the two-dimensional Gross--Neveu model at large $N$. The exact order-$1/N$ fermion self-energy in the massive vacuum is a finite real integral; ambiguities arise only after its large-momentum behavior is decomposed into products of perturbative Wilson coefficients and the matrix elements of the associated operators. A reduced integral isolates the leading renormalon and reveals a two-dimensional contour geometry in which the perturbative series is generated at the boundary corner where the exchanged momentum reaches the external hard scale, while its Stokes discontinuity is controlled by a distinct boundary critical point at zero exchanged momentum. After renormalizing the ultraviolet divergences of the condensate scale integral, we derive its compensating lateral ambiguity without fixing it by matching to the perturbative sector, distinguish the renormalon saddle from the apparent IR Landau pole, and obtain the reduced contour variables from the large-$N$ auxiliary-field path integral. The renormalon saddle is a boundary critical point of this reduced mode-space integral. The perturbative Wilson coefficient and the renormalized condensate thus define complementary relative cycles of the same reduced integrand whose complex tails cancel, reconstructing the original real integration cycle.
